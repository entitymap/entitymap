# Code of Conduct

EntityMap is a small open standard. We're going to act like it.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and welcoming. Many people coming to this repo will be new to formal specifications. Help them in, don't gatekeep.
- Disagree on the substance, not the person. Spec work is full of legitimate competing tradeoffs — name the tradeoff, don't characterise the other party.
- Take criticism gracefully. Yours and theirs.
- Use English in issues and PRs so the broadest set of contributors can read along.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, slurs, doxxing, sexualised language or imagery.
- Deliberately bad-faith arguing — arguments designed to exhaust rather than illuminate.
- Sustained disruption of discussion threads, working groups, or in-person events.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainers listed at the top of the [README](README.md). The maintainers will respond privately and take whatever action they judge proportionate — from a quiet word, through editing or removing comments, to banning persistent bad actors.

We do not promise neutrality between someone behaving badly and someone being harmed. We will side with the person being harmed.

## Scope

This applies in issues, PRs, discussions, and any in-person or online event organised under the EntityMap name.

This document is loosely modelled on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) but deliberately short.
